I'm back! This time I've compiled my favourite list of the best women's one day international matches! All of them have made me love this game even more! Please let me know in the comments what you think or add your own! Here are my picks for the 10 most thrilling women's ODI games so far:

  1. England Women vs India Women, 2022, Royal London ODI Lord's Cricket Ground - Highlights: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lt1HGm6dWUw - A low-scoring thriller with a dramatic finish. India won by 2 wickets with just 2 balls to spare.
  2. England Women vs India Women, 2022, Royal London ODI Trent Bridge - Highlights: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F87wLUNRxUs - Another close match between England and India, with India winning by 4 wickets with just 1 ball to spare.
  3. Australia Women vs India Women, 2021, ODI Series Gold Coast Cricket Ground - Highlights: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Py9wMbAxB1M - India ended Australia's world-record ODI winning streak with a thrilling 3-wicket victory.
  4. India Women vs Sri Lanka Women, 2023 - Asian Games 2023, Hangzhou, China - Highlights: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ - A high-scoring match with plenty of sixes and fours. India won by 9 wickets with 10 overs to spare.
  5. England Women vs New Zealand Women, 2023 - Highlights: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ - England won by 3 wickets, with the match going down to the wire.
  6. England Women vs Australia Women, 2022, ICC Women's Cricket World Cup Final - Highlights: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ - A rematch of the 2017 final, with England winning this time by 7 wickets.
  7. West Indies Women vs England Women, 2023 - Highlights: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ - A high-scoring match with plenty of wickets. West Indies won by 5 wickets with 3 overs to spare.
  8. India Women vs Pakistan Women, 2017, ICC Women's Cricket World Cup - Highlights: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ - An emotional match between two arch-rivals. India won by 95 runs.
  9. New Zealand Women vs Australia Women, 2021, ICC Women's Cricket World Cup - Highlights: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ - A close match that went down to the wire. Australia won by 1 wicket with just 1 ball to spare.
  10. England Women vs South Africa Women, 2017, ICC Women's Cricket World Cup - Highlights: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ - A match that went into Super Over. England won the Super Over by 1 run.

What do you think? Please let me know in comments if your own favourite matches, or make a new post with your own!
